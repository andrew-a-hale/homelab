# Avatar: The Last Airbender (2005) — 1080p is unavailable over usenet

Opened 2026-11-10 while trying to grab ATLA alongside The Legend of Korra.
Korra landed fine; ATLA did not, and the reason is not a bad post.

## Finding: title-wide takedown, ~10–15% of articles gone

Every animated-ATLA 1080p post refuses to start, all at the same health, across
different groups, encoders and post ages. That is a content-level takedown, not
one dead upload.

| Post | Age | Health | par2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Avatar The Last Airbender (2005) S01 1080p BluRay 10bit EAC3 2 0 x265-iVy` | 577d | 89.7 | 9.3% |
| `Avatar The Last Airbender S01 Bluray EAC3 2 0 1080p x265-iVy` | 1011d | 89.7 | — |
| `Avatar.The.Last.Airbender.S01E02…1080p.BluRay.x264-OFT` | 3mo | 89.8 | 10.0% (200 blocks / 1007 MB) |
| `Avatar.The.Last.Airbender.S01E01…1080p.FLAC.2.0.AVC.REMUX-FraMeSToR` | 3mo | 89.8 | — |
| `[SubsNoJutsu] … S01 (1080p BluRay AAC x265) [Batch]` | 8d | 85.4 | 0% |

NZBGet's gate is `HealthCheck=park` with a hidden ~89.9% critical threshold, so
all but the last are cancelled before a byte lands; `FAILURE/HEALTH`.

**Freshness is irrelevant** — an 8-day-old post is as holed as a 1011-day-old
one, and that fresh one sits under the gate too. **par2 cannot save it:** iVy
carries 9.3% redundancy against 10.3% missing, OFT carries 10.0% against 10.2%,
and the SubsNoJutsu batch ships no par2 at all. Forcing `HealthCheck=none`
buys a `FAILURE/UNPACK` after the whole download.

Control: The Legend of Korra (2012) 1080p BluRay x265-iVy, same provider, same
session — 100% health on all four seasons. So the provider and account are fine;
it is this title.

Do not bother with the 2026 movie or the 2024 Netflix show when searching —
`Avatar*.1080p` results are dominated by both, and neither is the animated
series. Animated episode titles ("The Boy in the Iceberg", "The Avatar
Returns") are how to tell them apart.

## Resolved 2026-11-10: the indexer, not the post

NZBGeek (lifetime sub, `pass show homelab/nzbgeek/apikey`) lists per-episode
re-ups the free indexers never show. `Avatar.The.Last.Airbender.S01E01…1080p.
BluRay.x264-nikt0` (9 months old, 1.3 GB) came back at **health 998/1000**,
downloaded `SUCCESS/PAR 991`, and carries English audio + English SDH subs.
The whole nikt0 set (61 episodes, ~80 GB) was queued from geek.

Lesson for the next title: "every post is holed" means every post *the free
indexers can see* is holed. Search geek with short queries before writing a
title off.

## Options, none taken yet

1. **Re-up watch** — a new upload of a 1080p encode would come with fresh
   articles. Cheapest option: re-run
   `./scripts/nzb-dl.sh search "Avatar The Last Airbender S01 1080p"` every so
   often and check the health column in `status`.
2. **Torrents** — Ralph's qBittorrent is not subject to usenet takedowns and
   the BluRay packs are well seeded. Same 1080p requirement, different pipe;
   needs a decision because this repo's media grabs assume usenet.
3. **720p over usenet** — untested, and against the 1080p-only rule.
