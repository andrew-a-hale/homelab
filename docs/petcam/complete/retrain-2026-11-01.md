## Round 2 (2026-11-01 evening) — willow detection gap: tiny/curled/high

**Symptom:** owner reports willow in frame, undetected. Live scans `top=0.00–0.02`.

**Cause:** she was perched on the cactus tree top — tiny, flat, dim (a stretched
loaf then a curled loaf, ~70–190 px). The corpus had only 13 willow boxes vs 68
pancake, and the model scored her 0.00–0.27 against `MIN_CONF=0.4`.

**Data, not threshold, first:** 7 frames captured live via `/capture` and saved
as willow (boxes measured per frame; the owner supplied the identity and one
hand-drawn box, plus 5 `_top=…_willow` labels from the owner's own `/label`
session). Corpus: 17 willow pairs. Two incremental retrains.

**Evidence after retrain:**
- The unseen original-failure frame: 0.00 → 0.27 → **0.42**, box and name right.
  Curled pose (trained-on): 0.78. Names on the corpus: 90 ok / 3 wrong / 3 missed.
- `MIN_CONF` 0.4 → **0.3**: false positives on the 109 hand-made negatives go
  1 → 2 (0.25 adds none — negatives are well-separated). The extra margin is
  worth it when a true case lands at 0.42. Selftest's harvest probe moved to
  0.2 to stay inside the near-miss band.

**Lesson:** the near-miss band (top ≥ 0.1) never queued these — hard misses
score below it, so `/live` **Capture** is the tool for "it's right there and
nothing happened" moments. Capture the pose while it lasts, box it, retrain.
