Retrained the petcam 2-class YOLOv8n (pancake/willow) detector and pushed it to Tom.

## What ran

  pairs pulled from Tom, 80/20 split.

  new model matches the old one everywhere except one frame where it finds an

  Negatives clean, no false positives. **Decision: keep the swap.**
  Note: A/B "truth" is the old model's auto-labels, so it inherently favours
  `.bak`; the extra detection was inspected and accepted.

(git checkout on Tom; a `petcam-detect.py.pre-git` stub is left in `~/`).

Fixed at the root: the restart command in `scripts/train-model.sh`, plus the
stale paths in `skills/petcam-retrain/SKILL.md` (restart, rollback, and the
importlib quality-gate snippet). The pkill bracket trick
